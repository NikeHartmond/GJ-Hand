Hansi Wimmer and Nike Hartmond are an Artist and scenographer duo who treat their working relationship like a romantic one.

Beyond their artistic practice, they are connected by shared reflections on aesthetics, production conditions of their own work and that of others, as well as commercial project and a shared political action (?). They examine accessibility from a sociocultural perspective and search for emancipatory knowledge and tricks they can apply in their own practice. Through their collaboration they develop a focus on the material and its manufacturing history.

To both their advantage and disadvantage they have very similar strengths, weaknesses and interests. Recently they committed to each other forever.
